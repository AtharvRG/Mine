## Resolution
Per Decision D-102 and D-102b, `android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM` is declared in `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`.
`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `VIBRATE`, and `WAKE_LOCK` remain strictly omitted.
